# AI Workflow Creator System Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Practical AI creator command-center for Douyin/TikTok: a vertical phone-first production board with a faceless operator feel, compact workflow cards, terminal/code snippets, bilingual captions, and a restrained AI-avatar intro/outro placeholder. The style should feel useful, fast, and credible rather than cinematic fantasy or generic SaaS marketing.

## Colors

- Canvas charcoal: `#11100E`, `#1B1A17`
- Warm paper panels: `#F4EAD8`, `#E6D7BF`
- Ink text: `#171411`, `#2C261E`
- Signal mint: `#20D6A2`, `#9AF0D4`
- Creator coral: `#FF6B57`, `#B7342A`
- Utility amber: `#FFCA4F`
- Deep interface green: `#18382F`

## Typography

- Interface and captions: system sans-serif, 700-900 for hooks and Chinese captions.
- Script details: system sans-serif, 500-700 with tabular numbers.
- English subtitle lines stay smaller than Chinese lines.
- Letter spacing stays `0`.

## Motion

- Fast but controlled slide-ins for workflow cards.
- Screen area gently pans to imply active operation.
- Caption beats pop in with short vertical movement and strong readability.
- Avatar intro/outro uses a simple framed placeholder if no HeyGen avatar render exists.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use generic gradient-orb decoration.
- Do not hide important text under platform UI safe zones.
- Do not make the avatar the whole video in v1.
- Do not use private screenshots, keys, or real account data.
- Do not make the palette a single blue/purple theme.
